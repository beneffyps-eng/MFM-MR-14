About Us

Our story, vision, and what we believe

Who We Are

MFM MRO 14 is a branch of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries. We exist to help people encounter God through prayer, the teaching of Scripture, and a life of holiness.

Our services are built around worship, the Word, and intercession. We welcome first-time visitors, families, and anyone seeking a place to grow in Christ.

Vision

To raise a people who pray, live holy lives, and walk in the victory Christ has given.

Mission

To preach the gospel, make disciples, and serve our community in love and truth.

Focus

Prayer, deliverance, holiness, and restoration — from Obadiah 1:17.

What We Believe

  • The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority.
  • Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • Prayer and holiness are essential to the Christian life.
  • The church is a family called to worship, serve, and make disciples.
